# Non-Guarantee and Uncertainty Language — Version 10.1

Implemented on 20 August 2026.

## Terminology changes

User-facing “margin of safety” and “MOS opportunity” wording is replaced with “valuation buffer” and “below valuation-buffer threshold.” A valuation buffer is explicitly described as an assumption-based price threshold, not protection from loss.

“Financial safety” and “governance safety” are replaced with “balance-sheet resilience” and “governance resilience.” Risk scores remain model indicators, not assurances.

## Dashboard and reports

A prominent dashboard banner states that outputs are model estimates, not Buy/Sell recommendations, and that valuation buffers do not guarantee loss protection. The PDF cover, executive summary, watchlists, alerts, batch outputs, assumptions, charts, and documentation use the updated language.

## Assessments

- “Below valuation-buffer threshold”
- “Below estimated fair value — buffer threshold not met”
- “Near estimated fair value”
- “Above estimated fair value”
- “Fallback valuation — high uncertainty”

No result is labeled safe, guaranteed, Buy, Strong Buy, or risk-free.
